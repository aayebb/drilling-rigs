How to use

Normal view: drag to orbit, right-drag (or two fingers) to pan, scroll to zoom. Fly freely with WASD, Q/E down/up, hold Shift to go fast. Use the left panel to fly between rigs and views. Click any part for an explanation.

Tool gallery: a display deck next to the jack-up with bits, BHA tools, handling tools, BOP parts, cementing and wellhead equipment (enlarged).

Subsurface: press Subsurface or a brown Wellbore button to see the geology below each rig: casing strings cut away to show cement and the open hole, directional and horizontal well paths, reservoir layers with gas / oil / water contacts. The drill string and bit in the hole follow the live bit depth when you drill or trip. Click any rock layer to learn about it.

Operations: Drill ahead, trip out / trip in, or drag the top-drive slider. On the jack-up, try Jack down / up. On the drillship, switch between the main and auxiliary well centers.

VR mode: press Enter VR on a WebXR headset (e.g. Meta Quest browser). Left controller holds the menu; point with the right controller and pull the trigger to press buttons or select parts. Right stick moves, left stick up/down & turn, hold grip to go faster.

Water depths are true scale; horizontal distances between rigs are compressed. Drill pipe is drawn thicker than reality so you can see it. Values are typical, for teaching.
